Everything Wikipedia knows within a short walk

Type in somewhere — a town, a street, a landmark, a postcode — or let your browser say where you are. Every Wikipedia article with coordinates inside a walk goes on the map.

Star articles to plan a walk past them, then open it in Google Maps.

Your location is never saved, and shared links only carry places you’ve typed.

Try Glasgow, Bath, Whitby or EH1 1YZ.
