Everything Wikipedia knows within half an hour on foot

Type in somewhere — a town, a street, a landmark, a postcode — or let your browser say where you are. Every Wikipedia article with coordinates inside a 30-minute walk goes on the map.

Your location is never saved, and shared links only carry places you’ve typed.

Try Glasgow, Bath, Whitby or EH1 1YZ.
